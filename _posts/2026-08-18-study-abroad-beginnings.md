There is a distinct blend of excitement and anxiety that comes with packing your entire life into two suitcases. As the departure date for my study abroad semester approaches, the reality of living in a completely different country is starting to set in.

For a long time, studying abroad felt like a distant concept—something I put on my college checklist but hadn't fully visualized. Now, with visa documents in hand, course selections finalized, and a plane ticket booked, it's very real.

### The Objectives

During my time abroad, I want to focus on three main things:
1.  **Cultural Immersion:** Stepping outside my comfort zone, practicing the language daily, and trying as much local food as possible.
2.  **Academic Growth:** Learning from a different academic system and taking courses that offer local perspectives on history and geopolitics.
3.  **Reflective Journaling:** Documenting my experiences here on **jme.log** so I can look back on how my perspectives change over the coming months.

### Geopolitical Observations from the Air

One of the classes I'm most excited about is *Global Political Geography*. Even the route my flight is taking reflects the complexities of modern borders, airspace rights, and international relationships. 

I plan to write more about these observations as my travels progress, blending personal experiences with academic insights.
